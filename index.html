    Send this link to a colleague. Everyone who installs it gets their own private, empty logbook — nothing is shared or synced between devices.

    Install on iPhone or iPad

    1. Open the link in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    4. Always open the logbook from the Home Screen icon.

    Install on Android

    1. Open the link in Chrome.
    2. Tap Install on the banner, or tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
    3. Open the logbook from its Home Screen icon.

    About and privacy

    Surgical Logbook

    Your logbook is stored only on this device, in this browser. There are no accounts, no analytics, no cloud sync and no tracking. After the first visit the app works fully offline and makes no network requests with your data.

    Log procedures only. Never enter patient names, hospital numbers, dates of birth or any other identifiers.

    Because data never leaves your device, export a JSON backup regularly — if you lose or reset the device, the backup is the only copy.
